Beagle Trivia

  1. Beagles have an excellent sense of smell.
  2. They were originally bred for hunting rabbits and hares.
  3. Beagles are friendly, curious and energetic dogs.
  4. They have long, floppy ears.
  5. Beagles enjoy companionship and regular exercise.
